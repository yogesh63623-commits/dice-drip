Our Philosophy

More Than Coffee. It's Game Time.

At The Dice & Drip, we believe the best memories happen around a table. Maybe it's the moment your friend realizes you've been bluffing for twenty minutes. Maybe it's a ridiculous argument over the rules of a legacy campaign. Or maybe it's simply two people catching up over single-origin coffee while a tactical board game rests quietly between them.

We designed our venue around real conversations: custom wide oak tables with recessed play surfaces, spill-safe beverage cup rings, grease-free gourmet shareables, and lighting tuned specifically to eliminate card glare.

☕ Ethically Sourced Coffee

Sourced from micro-lot farms, dialed in daily on our La Marzocco.
